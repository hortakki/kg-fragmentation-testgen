Subject: Pilot concern about international billing presentation

Hi Team,

Finance has a concern related to the pilot rollout for internationally billed equipment.

For a subset of high-value inventory, we may need a billing presentation approach that works more cleanly across more than one currency context. The current specification covers general pricing and billing expectations, but it does not go into much detail about how pilot-specific currency presentation or conversion rules should be handled in customer-facing outputs.

Before any implementation shortcut is taken, please clarify whether currency conversion, display format, and invoice currency are supposed to be handled as strict accounting rules, temporary pilot behavior, or only a presentation-layer convention. We want to avoid a situation where the operational display becomes simpler, but the customer-visible totals no longer align cleanly with the underlying financial records.
