Subject: Registration consent flow must be revised before launch

Hi All,

During the latest compliance review, Legal raised a blocking concern regarding how consent is collected in the registration journey.

At the moment, the current approach appears to bundle multiple legal acknowledgements too closely. For launch readiness, users need to express consent to the contractual terms and the personal-data-related notice in a clearly distinguishable way. A single combined acknowledgement is not sufficient for this purpose.

Please adjust the registration flow so that these approvals are captured independently and can be demonstrated separately if needed during audit.

This is a release-blocking compliance item.
