Subject: Escalation: exception handling for priority customers

Hi Dev Team,

Support keeps running into cases where a high-priority customer wants to secure an item even though the system currently treats the time window as blocked because of another pending request.

Operationally, the team is asking whether specially authorized staff should be able to intervene in those cases and resolve the conflict manually, rather than forcing the customer to wait for the ordinary availability flow to clear on its own. In practice this would mean allowing a priority booking path to take precedence over weaker, not-yet-confirmed demand.

Please do not treat this as confirmed product policy yet. We need guidance on whether this should remain a support-side exception process, or whether the booking logic itself is expected to support it.
