Subject: Warehouse handling concern for item identifiers

Hi All,

During the warehouse pilot, the team raised a usability concern around how item identifiers are handled on labels and during scanning.

In daily operations, staff tend to refer to the machine-readable identifier as the barcode or serial code rather than by the formal term used in the specification. The current business-oriented format may be less convenient for the scanning workflow than a more scanner-friendly representation.

For now, please review whether the warehouse-facing identifier should remain just an operational alias in the UI, or whether a separate machine-readable identifier is needed alongside the formal item identifier. The main goal is to reduce ambiguity for staff and avoid slowing down intake and return handling.
