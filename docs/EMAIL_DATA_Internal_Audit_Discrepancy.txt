Subject: Review findings on reporting consistency

Hi Dev Team,

During our recent reporting review, we found several consistency issues that need clarification before the reporting package can be considered reliable for audit use.

First, the financial outputs do not appear to align cleanly across all billing contexts. In some cases, the figures shown in reports, exported files, and billing records seem to reflect different presentation or calculation assumptions.

Second, the activity and audit views currently favor a privacy-preserving identifier style that makes operational review harder for non-technical auditors. We need to confirm whether this is the intended audit model, or whether authorized reviewers should have access to a controlled identity-resolution path.

Third, exported reporting artifacts do not always appear to match the same underlying interpretation used elsewhere in the platform, which raises a broader question about which data representation should be treated as authoritative for audit purposes.

Please review and confirm the intended source-of-truth model for report generation, export, and audit visibility.
